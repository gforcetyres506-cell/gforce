## 3. Service Offerings to Select in GBP

In your Google Business Profile dashboard under **Services**, add the following custom services:

1. **Mobile Tyre Fitting**
   - *Description:* On-site tyre fitting at your home or workplace using fully equipped mobile workshops.
2. **Puncture Assessment & Repair**
   - *Description:* Legal puncture repairs adhering strictly to British Standard BS AU 159 minor repair guidelines.
3. **Electronic Wheel Balancing**
   - *Description:* High-speed dynamic wheel balancing on our onboard computer balancers to ensure smooth, vibration-free driving.
4. **Emergency Mobile Callout**
   - *Description:* Urgent mobile tyre replacement for flat tyres and blowouts at roadside or home locations.
5. **New Rubber Valve Replacement**
   - *Description:* Replacement standard tubeless valves fitted with every new tyre, plus TPMS sensor service.
6. **Seasonal Tyre Swapping**
   - *Description:* Convenient switching between summer and winter tyres on your driveway.

## 4. Setting Service Areas

Under **Service Areas** in GBP:
- Select only the outward postal codes or local borough/town areas where you have active mobile dispatch coverage.
- Google allows up to 20 service areas.
- Examples based on your dispatch radius: e.g., specific postal districts or metropolitan areas within a 20–30 minute driving radius of your base.
- *Rule:* Google guidelines state service areas should not exceed a 2-hour driving radius from your business base.

## 6. Verification Steps for the Owner

1. Log into your Google account associated with the business at [google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business).
2. Choose **Video Verification** if prompted:
   - Google now commonly requires a continuous 1–2 minute video for Service Area Businesses.
   - Walk up to your mobile tyre fitting van showing the company branding/livery and registration plate.
   - Unlock the van doors to show the tyre fitting machine, balancing machine, and tools inside.
   - Show business documentation if available (van insurance certificate, invoices, or utility bill with the registered business name).
3. Once verified, ensure all contact numbers match the official business phone configured in your production website.
